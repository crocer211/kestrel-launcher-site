Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Kestrel Launcher is a local desktop application for Windows. We do not operate user-data collection servers, analytics trackers, or advertising networks.

1. Microsoft & Minecraft Authentication

When you sign in with a Microsoft account, Kestrel Launcher communicates directly from your device with official Microsoft Identity, Xbox Live, and Mojang/Minecraft Services endpoints to:

  • Authenticate your Microsoft account via OAuth 2.0.
  • Verify Minecraft: Java Edition game ownership.
  • Retrieve your Minecraft profile (username and UUID) and short-lived game session token.

To keep you signed in across sessions, both your Microsoft refresh token and your short-lived Minecraft session token are stored locally on your machine inside %APPDATA%\Kestrel\accounts.json, encrypted at rest using Windows DPAPI (scoped to your current Windows user account). Tokens are never sent to the developer or any third party.

2. Third-Party Content APIs

When you install loaders, search content, or check for updates, Kestrel Launcher makes direct requests from your device to the respective third-party services:

  • Mojang / Microsoft (game manifests, assets, authentication, and entitlement checks)
  • Modrinth & CurseForge (mods, modpacks, resource packs, and shaders)
  • Fabric, Quilt, Forge, & NeoForge Maven/meta servers (mod loader metadata and artifacts)
  • Adoptium (Eclipse Temurin Java runtime downloads)
  • authlib-injector (authlib-injector.yushi.moe — downloaded for offline profiles only to render local skins/capes)
  • GitHub (launcher updates and optional Horizon external engine releases)

Those requests are subject to each provider's own privacy policy.

3. Local Data

Instances, worlds, screenshots, logs, settings, and playtime statistics remain strictly on your local computer. You can remove your account or delete any instance data at any time from within the application or by deleting your local data folder.

4. Non-Affiliation

Kestrel Launcher is an independent third-party launcher. It is not an official Minecraft product and is not approved by or associated with Mojang AB or Microsoft Corporation.

5. Contact

Questions, bug reports, or privacy inquiries can be submitted via our GitHub Issues page.
